## Picking heuristics

If you want a quick rubric:

1. **Long-lived feature branch** — pick something you like *saying*. **speedbird, hayabusa, kuroshio, kagayaki, settebello, phoebe**.
2. **Quick hotfix** — short and snappy. **toki, sonic, viper, foxbat, habu, ulf**.
3. **Experimental / risky** — military codenames feel right. **warthog, prowler, growler, hokum, foxbat, fishbed**.
4. **Elegant** — European. **settebello, mistral, helvetia, cisalpino, caravelle**.
5. **Fast** — **sapsan, hayabusa, hayate, mallard, concorde**.
6. **Workhorse / reliable** — rolling stock models. **amfleet, superliner, electrostar, turbostar, tangara, waratah**.
7. **Refactor** — something steady. **kodama, hikari, shamrock**.

Keep a fleet within a theme if you can. **amfleet** next to **foxbat** next to **settebello** is going to make your brain do extra work.

## Don't pick these (just a heads-up, not a real warning)

- **main, master, trunk, head** — reserved or confusing
- **cassiopeia, hokutosei** — retired Japanese sleeper trains whose retirements were genuinely mourned; fine but heavy

Use whatever you want. These are vibes notes, not gates.
